Home

Every sanctioned app, in one place

Your sanctioned apps live on the home page. Tap one to start its ad break, and see live unlock timers at a glance.

  • Sort by the apps you open most
  • Drag an Express Pass onto an app to skip its ad break
Sanctions

Rules that fit each app

Every sanction is customizable, and setup takes under 30 seconds.

  • Unlock for 1 to 60 minutes; the app relocks automatically
  • Choose a 15, 30 or 60 second ad break
  • Get a relock warning at a time you choose
Insights

See your habits change

Weekly summaries show how you really use your sanctioned apps, with a recap every Sunday.

  • Total sessions and your most accessed apps
  • Time unlocked across your sanctioned apps
  • How often you cancel an ad break instead of unlocking
  • Each week compared with your recent average

Dynamic Sanctioning

Loosen or tighten any sanction for each day of the week, or snooze it entirely for the day.

Express Pass

In a hurry? Skip an ad break with an Express Pass. Hold up to two at a time, each recharging 24 hours after use. Turn them on in Settings.
